Trust is part of the interface

Know what is certain.
Know what is not.

Good research makes confidence, provenance, and gaps visible. When the evidence is weak, the research should say so.

01
Source provenance

Keep titles, domains, dates, and source types close to the claim they support.

02
Confidence in context

Separate cited evidence, estimates, and interpretation instead of flattening them into one answer.

03
Human judgement

Review important claims, assumptions, and limitations before relying on a conclusion.
