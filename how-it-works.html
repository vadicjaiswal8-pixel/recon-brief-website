HOW IT WORKS

From a research question to a structured report.

Good research is more than collecting information. It involves finding relevant sources, understanding what they say, comparing findings, and presenting the results in a useful form.

Recon Brief is designed to help organise this process into four steps.

QUESTION / CONTEXT / FINDINGS / REPORT

The research workflow

Four steps from
question to understanding.

Each stage helps keep the objective visible while information is gathered, assessed, and organised.

01
BEGIN WITH PURPOSE

Ask a question

Start with a specific topic, business problem, academic question, or research objective. A focused question helps establish what the investigation needs to answer.

Examples include:

  • What factors are influencing demand in a particular market?
  • How do competing companies differentiate themselves?
  • What opportunities and risks exist in an industry?
  • What evidence supports or challenges a research hypothesis?

The clearer the question, the easier it becomes to assess whether the resulting report addresses the intended objective.

02
FIND RELEVANT MATERIAL

Gather relevant sources

Recon Brief uses the research capabilities available in the current application to gather or work with relevant information. Depending on the enabled workflow, this may involve external research sources, AI services, or user-provided material where supported.

Source quality and coverage can vary. Sources may differ in authority, recency, relevance, and completeness. Where possible, original documents, official publications, and primary data should receive careful consideration.

Evaluate the materialNo single source type is automatically authoritative for every question. Consider whether each source is suitable for the subject and the decision at hand.
03
FOLLOW THE RESEARCH TRAIL

Understand the basis of the findings.

Evidence-oriented research should help users understand where findings come from and what the available material supports.

  • Assess whether a source actually supports the claim being made.
  • Consider the source's authority, relevance, and publication date.
  • Pay attention when credible sources disagree.
  • Distinguish observed results from estimates and projections.
  • Recognise gaps, assumptions, and limitations.

Source links do not automatically guarantee that a claim has been independently verified. Inspect original sources for important information.

04
ORGANISE WHAT YOU LEARNED

Turn research into something useful.

Recon Brief aims to organise research into structured outputs suitable for understanding a topic and communicating findings. Depending on the current application's implemented capabilities, these may include:

  • Structured market research reports.
  • Competitive intelligence and competitor analyses.
  • Company research.
  • Academic research material.
  • Research studies.
  • Presentation preparation material.

Structured research can support decision-making, but it does not replace independent judgement. Available output options depend on the current application.

Choose your level of detail

Choose the depth that
matches your question.

Different research questions require different levels of investigation. The current application provides three report-depth options.

01 / CONCISE

Quick Answer

A concise response for focused questions where a fast overview is sufficient.

02 / STRUCTURED

Standard Report

A structured report covering the core findings, supporting information, and analysis needed to understand the topic.

03 / COMPREHENSIVE

Deep Analysis

A more comprehensive investigation intended to provide broader evidence, comparisons, deeper reasoning, and discussion of limitations where available.

These descriptions explain the existing options. Available details and functionality depend on the current application.

Get more from your question

A clearer question leads to
a more useful investigation.

Give the research enough context to focus the work, then review the results with care.

01

Be specific

Define the topic, industry, company, geography, or time period when relevant.

02

Explain your objective

State whether you need an overview, a comparison, an evaluation, or a deeper investigation.

03

Provide context

Include relevant background, constraints, and assumptions.

04

Choose the appropriate depth

Use Quick Answer for concise questions, Standard Report for structured coverage, and Deep Analysis for a more comprehensive investigation.

05

Review the evidence

Check whether the sources are relevant, current, and supportive of the claims presented.

06

Treat uncertainty carefully

Look for disagreement between sources, distinguish estimates from observed results, and review the limitations before relying on conclusions.

!
Responsible research

Use research as a starting point for informed decisions.

AI-assisted research can help organise information and surface useful findings, but it is not a substitute for independent judgement. Sources may be incomplete, information may change, and conclusions may require additional verification.

Review important evidence, check consequential claims against original sources, and acknowledge limitations before relying on a report.
