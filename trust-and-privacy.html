01

Account control

Your account

  • You sign in with your email and password. Sign-in is handled by Supabase, and we never see or store your password ourselves.
  • You can delete your account and all saved data yourself in Settings → Delete your account. It permanently removes your reports, projects and sources, and your sign-in record.
  • You can also delete individual reports at any time.
02

Stored data

What we store

  • Your email address and the reports and projects you create. A report includes what you entered, the finished report, and the list of sources it used, with short excerpts of their text.
  • Your email is stored with each report.
  • Usage counters used to prevent abuse. They use your account ID and a one-way scrambled (hashed) version of your IP address, never the IP address itself.
  • Saved data stays in your account until you delete it. We don't yet delete data automatically.
  • Your data is protected by database rules: each account can read and change only its own data.
03

Local processing

Files you upload

  • Files are read inside your browser. We never upload or store the original file.
  • The text extracted from a file is sent to the AI service to write your report. Short excerpts of the passages used may appear in your saved report.
04

Data handlers

Who handles your data

To produce a report, Recon Brief uses these services:

  • Supabase: sign-in and storage of your account data.
  • Vercel: hosts the app. Our server logs record error codes, never your questions or reports.
  • AI providers (Google Gemini, Groq, OpenRouter): they receive what is needed to write your report: your inputs, excerpts of web sources, and excerpts of uploaded files. They never receive your email or account ID.
  • Tavily (web search): receives search queries built from what you type, such as a company name, market or question. It never receives your email or account ID.
  • Google: your browser loads fonts from Google Fonts and website icons from Google's icon service, so Google can see your IP address and which source websites appear in your reports.

Recon Brief doesn't train AI models on your data. Provider-specific data handling is governed by the applicable terms and privacy documentation of each provider.

Analytics: the public Recon Brief marketing website currently uses Google Analytics 4. Google may receive website usage information when those pages load the Google Analytics service. This analytics disclosure applies to the marketing website; it does not describe the separate authenticated application's data processing.

05

Browser-side data

What stays on your device

  • Your sign-in session, and the last project you opened.
  • A saved copy of a report in progress, kept for up to 2 hours so a failed run can resume. It is removed when the run finishes.
  • Recent search results, kept for 24 hours so repeated reports don't repeat searches.

Clearing your browser's site data removes all of this. Our code sets no cookies.

06

Safeguards

How we protect it

  • Sign-in is required for every server function.
  • Limits on usage and request size per account and per network address, to prevent abuse.
  • Only approved AI providers can be used.
  • Users never see raw technical errors from providers.
  • All report text is safely escaped, and source links must start with HTTP or HTTPS.
  • Security headers block the site from being embedded in other pages and restrict browser features.
  • The libraries that read your files are hosted by us, not loaded from outside servers.
  • No administrator-level database key is used by the app, so the database rules always apply.
07

Evidence and limits

How we keep reports honest

  • Every source is numbered and listed with a label showing how reliable it is.
  • An Evidence Check compares each figure in a report with the text of the source it cites. It also flags figures with the wrong year or source, and separates facts from interpretation and assumptions.
  • Projections are calculated by the app from stated numbers, not invented by the AI.
  • Searches that failed, and gaps in the evidence, are stated in the report.
LIMITS

Reports are AI-assisted and can contain mistakes. The Evidence Check compares numbers with retrieved text but cannot confirm that a source's meaning supports a claim. Check important facts before relying on a conclusion.
