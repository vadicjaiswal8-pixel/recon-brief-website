ABOUT RECON BRIEF

Research that helps you understand, compare, and decide.

Recon Brief is an AI-assisted research and intelligence platform designed to help turn research questions into structured, evidence-oriented reports.

Whether you are exploring a market, analysing competitors, researching a company, or preparing an academic project, Recon Brief aims to make research easier to organise, understand, and use.

Our goal is simple: help people move from a question to a clearer understanding of the subject, supported by relevant sources and analysis.

Our approach

Research should be more
than a collection of information.

Useful research requires more than gathering facts. Information needs context, findings need supporting evidence, and conclusions need to acknowledge uncertainty.

01

Start with a question

Define the topic, problem, or decision you want to investigate.

02

Bring relevant information together

Organise relevant information and research material available through the platform's existing capabilities.

03

Connect findings with evidence

Present findings alongside supporting material so users can better understand the basis of the conclusions.

04

Produce a structured output

Organise the research into an output suited to the selected research workflow and level of detail.

!

AI-generated analysis can contain errors or gaps. Important facts, figures, quotations, and recommendations should be checked against original sources before being relied upon.
