ABOUT RECON BRIEF

Research that helps you understand, compare and decide.

Recon Brief is an AI-assisted research and intelligence platform for people who need to investigate a company, understand a market or make sense of a competitive landscape.

It helps organise research questions, relevant information and analysis into structured reports designed to make findings easier to examine. Its approach combines a unified research workflow, inspectable evidence and flexible report depth.

Recon Brief is designed for founders, business professionals, investors and strategy teams who need a more structured way to investigate important questions.

What makes the approach useful

Research should be
inspectable and usable.

Recon Brief combines three differentiators: inspectable evidence, one research workflow, and flexible report depth.

01 / EVIDENCE

Inspect the evidence

Follow important findings back to the source information presented in the report. Examine supporting material, identify uncertainty and decide what deserves further checking.

02 / WORKFLOW

One research workflow

Investigate a company, explore a market, compare competitors, organise academic research or investigate a custom question through one research workflow.

03 / DEPTH

Choose your report depth

Start with a Quick Answer, use a Standard Report for structured coverage, or select Deep Analysis when the question calls for a more comprehensive investigation.

!

AI-generated outputs can contain errors. Sources may be outdated or inconsistent, so important claims should be checked against the supporting material.
