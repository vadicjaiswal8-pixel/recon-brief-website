ABOUT RECON BRIEF

Research that helps you understand, compare, and decide.

Recon Brief is an AI-assisted research and intelligence platform designed to help organize a research question, relevant material, and analysis into a structured, evidence-oriented working output.

It is intended for people exploring a market, comparing competitors, researching a company, preparing an academic project, or organizing a study.

The approach keeps the question, supporting material, interpretation, uncertainty, and limitations close enough for a reader to review before relying on a conclusion.

Our approach

Research should be more
than a collection of information.

Useful research requires more than gathering facts. Information needs context, findings need supporting evidence, and conclusions need to acknowledge uncertainty.

01

Start with a question

Define the topic, problem, or decision you want to investigate.

02

Bring relevant material together

Gather the information that fits the question, and keep its source, date, scope, and limitations in view.

03

Connect findings with evidence

Present findings alongside supporting material so users can better understand the basis of the conclusions.

04

Shape a structured output

Organize the research into a form that makes the findings, supporting material, assumptions, and open questions easier to review.

!

AI-assisted analysis can contain errors or gaps. Use the Recon Brief Evidence Review Framework to review important sources, claims, uncertainty, and limitations before relying on a conclusion.
