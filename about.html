Who we are

About Recon Brief

Recon Brief is an AI-assisted research and competitive intelligence platform. It is designed to help people conduct structured research, evaluate information and produce reports in which findings can be traced back to their evidence.

The focus is research quality: a clear question, organised sources, careful analysis and a report you can inspect. It is built to support your judgement, not to replace it.

Research quality

Clear questions, organised information and careful analysis.

Evidence-backed insights

Findings stay connected to the material that supports them.

Source transparency

Sources are listed and labelled so you can review them yourself.

Structured analysis

Patterns, comparisons, opportunities and risks are set out in order.

Actionable intelligence

Reports are written to support decisions, with limitations stated.

Why Choose Recon Brief?

Recon Brief is built for people who need to understand a company, a market or a set of competitors, and who want to see how a conclusion was reached.

Structured research workflows

A consistent path from question to report keeps the work organised.

Evidence-backed reporting

Claims in the report point to the evidence behind them.

Source transparency

Business-focused analysis

Multiple research categories

Different levels of research depth

What we stand for

Our Unique Value Proposition

Research you can inspect. Intelligence you can act on.

Research you can inspect means the work is open to review. Sources are shown, claims are tied to evidence and limits are stated, so you can check what a conclusion rests on.

Intelligence you can act on means the findings are organised for decisions: patterns, comparisons, opportunities and risks set out clearly, so the next step is easier to judge.

A little more context about Recon Brief and the kinds of research it is designed to support.

What is Recon Brief?

Recon Brief is an AI-assisted research and intelligence platform that helps organise research into structured reports, bringing relevant evidence and analysis together to make findings easier to understand.

Who is Recon Brief for?

Recon Brief is designed for students, researchers, business professionals, entrepreneurs, and others who need to investigate a topic and organise their findings into a structured output.

What kinds of research can I organise?

Recon Brief is intended to support Company Research, Market Research, Competitive Intelligence, Academic Research, and Custom Research. Available functionality may depend on the current application and research task.
