What company research should clarify

Understand a company
beyond its headline numbers.

Coverage depends on the research question, the company and the available sources. Keep reported facts distinct from estimates, interpretations and open questions.

01 / OVERVIEW

Company overview

Clarify the business, its scope, relevant entities and the context needed to interpret the investigation.

02 / MODEL

Business model

Examine how the company describes its products, customers, channels, operations and sources of value.

03 / POSITION

Market position

Place the company within a defined category, geography, customer segment and time period.

04 / OFFERING

Products and differentiation

Compare current offers and stated differentiators without treating promotional language as proof of performance.

05 / SIGNALS

Growth signals and financial information

Review reported developments, disclosed figures and available indicators in their source and date context.

06 / LIMITS

Risks, uncertainties and open questions

Make missing information, conflicting accounts, assumptions and questions for further investigation visible.

From company name to structured research

Follow the question
through the work.

A company investigation becomes easier to review when each stage keeps the objective, source material, analysis and limitations in view.

01 / DEFINE

Define the question

State what you need to understand about the company, decision or comparison.

02 / RESEARCH

Gather relevant information

Collect material that fits the question and record its source, date, scope and limitations.

03 / EVIDENCE

Examine the evidence

Check what supports each important finding and where sources disagree or remain incomplete.

04 / ANALYZE

Analyse the findings

Compare patterns, signals and differences while keeping observations distinct from interpretation.

05 / REPORT

Organise the report

Bring findings, evidence, assumptions, limitations and open questions into a structure a reader can review.

Check whether the source actually supports the finding, verify figures and distinguish reported information from estimates or interpretation.

Company research questions

Frequently asked
questions.

Practical context for planning and reviewing a company investigation.

01What is a company research tool?⌄

A company research tool assists with organising information about a business, its products, market context, reported developments and supporting sources into a structured research output.

02How is company research different from competitor analysis?⌄

Company research focuses on understanding one business in context. Competitor analysis compares particular businesses, their offers, positioning and the forces shaping their market. The two can overlap.

03Can Recon Brief research private companies?⌄

Private-company information may be limited. Coverage depends on the available sources, the research question and what the company or other publishers disclose publicly.

04Are all company findings verified?⌄

No. Citations do not guarantee accuracy. Important findings, figures and claims should be checked against supporting material and reviewed for uncertainty, conflicting sources and gaps.

05Can I choose a short or detailed report?⌄

The available report depth may include Quick Answer, Standard Report and Deep Analysis. Availability and detail depend on the current application and research task.

Company research is not a substitute for financial, legal or investment due diligence.
