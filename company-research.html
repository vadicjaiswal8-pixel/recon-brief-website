Inspect the evidence behind the findings

Keep the business story
open to examination.

Research findings should be reviewed in context. Check important figures and claims against original material, and note uncertainty or missing information.

R

Review support, uncertainty and gaps.

Where supporting material is available, check whether it supports the finding. Review figures in context and distinguish reported information from estimates or interpretation.

When sources conflict, examine their dates, definitions, methods and incentives rather than blending them into a single confident statement.

Identify information gaps and the next question that could change the conclusion. For a practical review framework, see the Research Methodology.

Company research questions

Frequently asked
questions.

Practical context for planning and reviewing a company investigation.

01What is a company research tool?⌄

A company research tool helps organise an inquiry about a business and its context. Recon Brief’s setup asks for a company and scope, then presents a structured, four-stage company research workflow.

02How is company research different from competitor analysis?⌄

Company research focuses on understanding one business in context. Competitor analysis compares particular businesses, their offers, positioning and the forces shaping their market. The two can overlap.

03Can Recon Brief research private companies?⌄

Private-company information may be limited. Coverage depends on the available sources, the research question and what the company or other publishers disclose publicly.

04Are all company findings verified?⌄

No. Important findings and figures should be reviewed carefully. Where supporting material is available, check claims against it and note uncertainty, conflicting information and gaps.

05Can I choose a short or detailed report?⌄

The current Company Research setup offers Quick — focused answer, Standard — balanced report, and Deep Analysis — comprehensive report.

Company research is not a substitute for financial, legal or investment due diligence.
